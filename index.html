A Prayer for You

Father, I bring Ann before You tonight — the one who makes my world gentler. Cover her with Your peace, the kind that surpasses all understanding. Let Your face shine upon her and be gracious to her; give her rest when she is tired, joy that does not fade, and strength for every mountain ahead.

Surround her with friends who truly love her, dreams that honor You, and favor in everything her hands touch. Guard her going out and her coming in, and may she always know — beyond any doubt — how deeply she is loved, by You and by me.

Amen

Yours, with all my heart,
